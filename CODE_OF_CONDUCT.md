# Code of Conduct

We want a technically rigorous and welcoming project.

## Expected

- Discuss evidence, trade-offs, and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, accessibility, cultural differences, and contributor boundaries.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Keep security reports and private data out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential requests, or deliberate disruption.
- Fabricated testing, identities, contributions, metrics, or endorsements.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or reject content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented privately. Until a public contact is established, report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel rather than a public issue.
